AI Chief of Staff

Your personal AI advisor on Microsoft 365. No coding. No admin access. One prompt.

How to get started:
Copy the setup prompt below and paste it into M365 Copilot at microsoft365.com/copilot or in Teams.

That's it. Your first useful insight arrives in under 5 minutes — before you commit to the full setup.

Want to build a dedicated agent? See the Agent Instructions section below.

What It Does

Five interactive modes, each designed for a specific part of your day:

Morning Briefing — Your three priorities for today, calendar conflicts, and one thing slipping that you haven't asked about.
Meeting Prep — Before any meeting: who you're meeting, what they care about, your goal, talking points, and what to listen for.
Capture Session — After a meeting or conversation: log decisions, tasks, and intelligence. Everything gets connected to your priorities.
Strategic Brainstorm — Think through a decision with an advisor who knows your strategy, people, and landscape. Trade-offs, not platitudes.
Maintenance — Weekly review: what progressed, what's stale, which relationships need attention, and where your priorities doc needs updating.

The Habit Stack

Email hook: An automated morning briefing lands in your inbox before you start your day.

Daily session: You open Copilot, say "good morning," and get three actions tailored to your actual priorities and calendar.

Compounding system: Every meeting prep, captured decision, and logged signal makes the next briefing smarter. The more you use it, the more it knows.

Two Paths

Path 1: Get Started Now

Copy the setup prompt and paste it into M365 Copilot. It will interview you to build your strategic context, then start working as your Chief of Staff immediately.

Click the Copy full prompt button at the bottom-right of this page.

Time: 30 minutes for the initial interview. Then it's ready.

Path 2: Create Your Own Agent

If your M365 Copilot supports agent creation (look for "Agents" in the sidebar), you can build a dedicated Chief of Staff agent with a persistent persona.

  1. Open M365 Copilot and look for Agents or Create
  2. See AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md in the repo for the complete agent persona and build steps
  3. Add your OneDrive knowledge docs as knowledge sources
  4. Start using it daily

Time: 1-2 hours including the onboarding interview.

Scheduled Prompts
