## Proficiency Levels

- **Learning**: Aware of the skill, actively developing, needs support
- **Competent**: Can work independently, delivers reliably, may need guidance on edge cases
- **Expert**: Teaches others, handles complex/ambiguous problems, trusted authority

## Gap Analysis

### Step 1: Identify what the team needs

Map your roadmap to required skills. For each initiative in the next 12 months:
- What skills are required?
- At what proficiency level?
- How many people need that skill?

### Step 2: Compare needs vs. current state

| Skill | Needed (# people @ level) | Have Today | Gap | Priority |
|-------|---------------------------|------------|-----|----------|
| ML Engineering | 3 @ Competent | 1 Competent, 1 Learning | 1-2 people | Critical |
| Stakeholder Mgmt | All leads @ Competent | 2 of 4 leads | 2 people | High |
| Cloud (AWS) | 2 @ Expert | 1 Expert | 1 person | Medium |

### Step 3: Classify gaps

- **Critical**: Blocks a funded initiative in the next 6 months. Must close immediately.
- **High**: Needed for the 12-month roadmap. Plan now, close within 2 quarters.
- **Medium**: Important for team growth but not blocking anything. Address opportunistically.
- **Nice-to-have**: Future capability. Track but don't invest actively.

## Closing Gaps

### Hire
- Best for: Critical gaps where no internal candidate can ramp fast enough
- Watch out for: Hiring for a skill you can't evaluate. Bring in an expert to help interview
- Timeline: 2-4 months from req to start date, optimistically

### Train
- Best for: High/Medium gaps where existing team members have adjacent skills
- Options: Online courses, vendor certifications, conference workshops, internal lunch-and-learns
- Key principle: Training without application is wasted. Pair training with a real project
- Timeline: 1-3 months for competency jump, depends on starting point

### Pair
- Best for: Accelerating someone from Learning to Competent
- How: Assign a Competent/Expert team member as a working partner (not just a reviewer)
- Key principle: Pairing is not mentoring — both people are doing the work together
- Timeline: Ongoing, expect measurable improvement in 4-8 weeks

### Contract/Consult
- Best for: Short-term critical gaps, specialized skills you don't need permanently
- Watch out for: Knowledge walking out the door when the contract ends. Always pair contractors with internal staff
- Timeline: Days to weeks to engage

## Principles

- **T-shaped people are more valuable than specialists**: Deep in one area, competent across several
- **Leadership skills compound**: A great analyst who can't communicate findings has a ceiling. Invest in stakeholder management early
- **Review quarterly**: Skills, projects, and strategy shift. Update the matrix every quarter
- **Make it visible**: Share the matrix (with permission) so the team sees growth paths and can self-direct
- **Don't hoard talent**: If someone is ready for a bigger role and you don't have one, help them find it — they'll remember, and your reputation for developing people will attract more talent
